# Content collection and launch checklist

## Before building personal content

- Confirm recipient's preferred name, birthday date, whether milestone age should appear, primary device/browser and desired privacy audience.
- Confirm who owns ongoing curation and account recovery help. Obtain actual commitment, not assumed goodwill.
- Ask whether she would enjoy receiving family updates; do not make her a compulsory content creator.
- Identify family contributors and who can approve each photograph, message or story. Permission from uploader alone may not resolve other people's privacy/rights.

## Practical contribution request

Ask each willing relative for: 2–3 favourite approved photographs; one short caption per image explaining the genuine occasion; one written birthday message or 30–90 second audio recording; optional genuine memory; explicit intended audience and restrictions. Requests are drafts for Ed to send: this pack does not authorise contacting anybody.

For each item record: contributor, supplied names/caption, date and precision or unknown, actual permission/rights basis, included people, sharing/download restrictions, alt text, transcript if audio, sensitivity/resurfacing preference. Do not guess relationships, dates or location from an image. Do not write invented recollections to make a page feel complete. AI may assist formatting only, with factual editorial approval.

## Seed targets

Suggested complete gift: 20–30 chosen images, five personal wishes, three genuine memories, a supplied dedication and several finished book chapters. These are optional quality targets, not release quotas. A smaller carefully edited book is better than filler. Optional crossword requires approved personal clue facts; picture mysteries require true answers and fair hints; timeline requires honest dates; calendar requires actual organiser-approved events.

## Asset register schema

`asset_reference, contributor, type, source_file, caption_or_message, named_people_supplied, date_value, date_precision, permission_basis, permitted_audience, download_allowed, sensitivity_note, resurface_allowed, alt_text, transcript, editorial_status, reviewer, approved_at`.

Store register privately; do not publish source paths, private permission notes or contributor email addresses. Originals need backups only within agreed retention. Operational access, deletion/withdrawal and signed-link expiry described in plain language.

## Gift reveal

- Populate and publish approved content; remove every demo record.
- Verify signed-out visitor and second-family isolation, RLS/private assets, passwordless/session recovery and mobile playback.
- Test actual recipient device or equivalent and internet connection.
- Add bookmark; demonstrate puzzle, family update and text reply without requiring contribution.
- Provide a simple printed card with site URL; QR optional, never the only route.
- Keep a usable birthday book when updates are quiet; no guilt messaging.

## Actual user trial

Ask a willing recipient/comparable puzzle enthusiast to start a puzzle, change difficulty, resume progress, find a photograph and leave a text reply. Observe hesitation and mistakes without directing steps. Include large-text and keyboard checks separately. Record device, date, tasks, observations and changes. Agent walkthroughs are simulated, not observed user research.

## Maintenance

Curator checks submissions and failed uploads; chooses a realistic weekly/fortnightly update or monthly edition cadence. Backups scheduled and restore verified, dependencies updated, storage limits monitored, member departures/revocations handled. Quiet feed is valid; do not manufacture updates. Review download/privacy settings with contributors. Make an exit/export plan so the gift can remain accessible if hosting stops.
