# Word Families: real editorial deduction upgrade

## Before → after

The supplied starter board uses birds, instruments, gemstones and herbs; it is valid but tutorial-grade for an experienced solver. The second uses teeth, footwear, ROOM compounds and punctuation. These are two finite examples, not an ongoing high-challenge editorial operation. Upgrade category quality and the complete selection engine; changing tile colours alone cannot create depth. Keep both originals as regression fixtures.

## Playable state machine

Load a shuffled identifier permutation and the pinned solution groups. Toggle up to four terms; display selected count; clear or shuffle without losing selection. Submit compares an unordered selected identifier set with each unsolved group. A correct group moves into its labelled explanation panel. A distinct wrong combination costs one mistake; repeating an identical wrong set does not. Optional “one away” means exactly three terms belong to an intended remaining group. After four mistakes, offer continue, hints or full explanation; continued play is marked assisted, never forcibly abandoned.

Completion presents all explanations, solved/missed groups, error count and assistance. A new archive or practice run is independent of the immutable daily result. Restore selections, solved groups, submitted wrong sets, board order and assistance. The engine needs actual group membership and transitions, matching Hexabble’s deterministic accepted/rejected action model; a generic toast must not stand in for evaluating the selected terms.

## Genuine difficulty and review

Gentle normally has twelve terms in three direct categories; sixteen-term starter fixture remains a specific tutorial test. Standard mixes direct semantic categories with fair linguistic constructions. Expert introduces controlled overlaps, alternative senses, shared prefixes or transformations that require considering the whole partition. Challenge arises from plausible competing hypotheses that evidence resolves, not unknown trivia or arbitrary association.

Use two independent editors who first solve without the answer sheet, then actively propose rival groups and complete partitions. Automated exact-cover checks can verify that the intended groups use every term once; they cannot establish semantic uniqueness. Reject or revise a coherent alternative partition. An intentionally multi-answer edition would need separate scoring rules and explicit messaging, and is not the default launch design. Include explanations giving full compounds or transformations, and record tested distractor interpretations in reviewer notes.

Tiles are not necessarily ordinary dictionary words: FULL STOP is a valid multiword term. Apply locale, factual and editorial checks rather than mechanically filtering every tile through the spelling list. Validate label length and mobile readability. Protect unseen answers in public puzzle payloads when spoiler resistance is promised.

## States and acceptance

Design loading, tutorial, selection, underfilled selection, correct group, wrong group, repeated wrong group, optional one-away, budget exhausted, assisted continuation, full reveal, completion, result, restart practice, archive and save failure. Use selection markings beyond colour and never shrink long terms unreadably. Focus remains predictable when solved tiles move.

Acceptance: select CRANE with the three other birds and solve; submit exactly three terms and consume no mistake; resubmit an identical wrong four-set and consume no additional mistake; shuffle preserves identifiers and selection; FULL STOP occupies one tile; all sixteen terms are consumed once at completion. Four misses must expose a working continue route. Editors must reject at least one deliberately ambiguous test board, proving the editorial gate is operational. Pilot expert boards with experienced solvers and distinguish a difficult fair connection from an unfair author-only rationale.
